CyclePath Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

CyclePath is a cycling application that helps users generate, save, and navigate cycling routes. This Privacy Policy explains what information CyclePath collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CyclePath, the application may process:

  • Location data: your device location to generate routes, display your position on the map, and track navigation progress.
  • Account information: information such as your name and email address when you create or use an account.
  • Profile information: information you choose to add to your profile.
  • Route and ride information: saved routes, distance, duration, speed, and ride history.
  • Profile images: an image you select for your profile. Profile images are stored locally within the application on your device.
  • Technical information: limited diagnostic information needed to maintain application reliability and security.

How We Use Information

CyclePath uses information to:

  • Generate cycling routes based on your location and requested distance.
  • Display maps, route instructions, and navigation information.
  • Calculate ride distance, duration, and speed.
  • Save routes and ride history for your use.
  • Provide account and profile functionality.
  • Improve application performance, security, and reliability.

Location Data

Location access is required for route generation and navigation features. Location data is used while those features are active. You can withdraw location permission at any time through your Android device settings, although some CyclePath features may no longer work.

Data Storage and Service Providers

CyclePath uses trusted third-party services for authentication, cloud profile data, maps, and route services. Saved routes, ride history, and other application data may be stored locally on your device. Profile images are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to cloud storage by CyclePath.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained while it is needed to provide the application features you use. You can remove saved routes, ride history, and profile images through the application where those options are available. You may also request account or personal data deletion by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

CyclePath is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by CyclePath. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the CyclePath project owner through the official GitHub profile: https://github.com/awstine
